# UAE follow list: employers to follow on LinkedIn (manual task for the owner)

These UAE employers are relevant to Java backend roles but use hiring systems JobRadar does not
read (own portals, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle, Avature, iCIMS or unidentified), so JobRadar
cannot fetch their jobs directly (`docs/research/uae-employer-hiring-systems-2026-10-31.md`).

**What to do (owner):** follow each company on LinkedIn and create a **company job alert**
(LinkedIn → company page → Jobs → "Create job alert", filtered to Software / Engineering, UAE),
or include the company in a keyword alert such as "Java" + company name. Their jobs then arrive
in Gmail as LinkedIn alert emails, which JobRadar already reads (alert email + one-tap; apply on
their site). Check off each company below when its alert exists.

Relevance is our editorial judgement (2026-10-31), based on each employer's digital, platform
or engineering work in the UAE; it is not verified against their current openings.
