# India follow list: employers to follow on LinkedIn (manual task for the owner)

These India employers are relevant to Java backend roles, but the probe of 2026-10-31
(`config/company_candidates/india.txt`) found no readable board for them on Greenhouse, Lever,
Ashby, Workable or SmartRecruiters. They use their own career portals or other hiring systems,
so JobRadar cannot fetch their jobs directly.

**What to do (owner):** follow each company on LinkedIn and create a **company job alert**
(LinkedIn → company page → Jobs → "Create job alert", filtered to Software / Engineering,
India), or add the company to a keyword alert such as "Java" + company name. Naukri, Instahyre
and Foundit company alerts work the same way. Their jobs then arrive as alert emails, which
JobRadar already reads (alert email + one-tap; apply on their site). Tick each company when its
alert exists.

Relevance and tiers are our editorial judgement (2026-10-31), based on each employer's known
backend engineering in India and the use of Java/JVM in its stack; they are not verified against
current openings. Real-money gaming companies (Dream11, MPL, Games24x7, Junglee, Zupee, WinZO)
are left out: add them only if you want that industry (see `config/industries.yaml`).
